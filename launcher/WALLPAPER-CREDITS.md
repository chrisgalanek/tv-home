# Wallpaper credits

Two sets, chosen with `wallpaper_set` in `launcher/res/values/config.xml`. Every picture comes from
Wikimedia Commons under a free licence (public domain, CC0 or CC BY / CC BY-SA), credited below
with its photographer and licence. The works themselves are centuries old and in the public domain;
the credit is for the photograph.

## Art (`launcher/assets/wallpapers/art/`)

Historic Christian paintings, sculpture, stained glass, mosaics, icons and churches. Captions shown
on the TV come from `art/captions.tsv`. Wide works are cropped to 16:9; taller ones fill the screen
height against the right edge and fade into a deep tone taken from the picture.
